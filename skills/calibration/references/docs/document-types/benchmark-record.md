# Benchmark Record

Document reproducible performance, numerical consistency, capacity, or scale
measurements and the claims they support. Include enough evidence for another
contributor to reproduce the measurement, inspect its output, and understand
the limits of its conclusions.

- Follow the repository's benchmark-record location. If none is defined, use
  `docs/contributing/`.
- Follow project measurement and reproducibility rules.
- State the question, workload, inputs, environment, and versions. For
  comparisons, identify the baseline and comparison method.
- Define metrics, protocol, repetitions, and the random seed where relevant.
- Preserve the output path or artifact needed to inspect the result.
- Report results with uncertainty, limitations, and any failed or skipped
  checks.
- State the conditions under which the result remains comparable or should
  be rerun.
